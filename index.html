About Us

Patliputra Guns Shooting Academy

Founded on February 24, 2022 by Chetan Anand with support from the Bihar State Rifle Association, Patliputra Guns Shooting Academy has grown into a leading shooting institution in Bihar. With training centres in Digha — Patna, Kankarbagh — Patna, and Ranchi — Coming Soon, PGSA trains budding and seasoned shooters through disciplined coaching, safe range practices, and Olympic-standard digital target systems.

Project Khojj

A Talent Hunt for Bihar's Future Shooters

Project Khojj is PGSA's awareness and talent-identification mission for young athletes. It introduces shooting as an Olympic discipline, builds confidence and discipline, and creates a pathway for promising students to receive structured training.

01

Awareness Camps

School and college outreach introduces students to shooting sports, safety, and athlete pathways.

02

Talent Identification

Promising students are identified through structured sessions and guided toward focused training.

03

Athlete Development

Selected athletes can progress into disciplined coaching, competition readiness, and long-term performance support.

Ganguly Consultancy supports PGSA through coaching expertise and Project Khojj execution, while PGSA remains the academy and primary training destination for shooters.
